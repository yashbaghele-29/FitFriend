🌟 4 Golden Rules for Eating Well:

  1. Drink 1 glass of water before each meal: Helps digestion and prevents overeating.
  2. Eat until 80% full: Give your stomach 15 minutes to signal fullness to the brain.
  3. Include protein in every main meal: Keeps you energetic and prevents sugar cravings.
  4. Don't skip dinner: Keep it light, eat 2 hours before bed for sound sleep.
